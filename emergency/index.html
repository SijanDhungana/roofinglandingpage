While You Wait

What To Do Right Now

A few simple steps can limit the damage before we arrive. No upsell — just what genuinely helps.

1

Move valuables & catch the water

Move furniture and valuables out of the drip path and put down a bucket or bin to contain it.

2

Relieve a bulging ceiling

If the ceiling is bulging, carefully pierce the low point with a screwdriver to drain it in a controlled spot. A bulge means trapped water — and a bigger collapse later.

3

Photograph everything

Take photos of the leak and any damage before cleanup — you’ll want them for your insurance claim.

4

Stay off the roof

Wet shingles are the leading cause of DIY roofing injuries. Leave the roof to us — then call and we’ll be on site in 2–4 hours.
