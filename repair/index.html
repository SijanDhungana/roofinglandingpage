What We Fix

Common Leak Sources We Repair

Finding the real source is what separates a lasting repair from a patch that fails next season.

Chimney flashing

Cracked or lifted flashing where the chimney meets the roof is one of the most common leak points. We re-seal or replace it properly.

Skylight seals

Skylights leak as their seals age and shift. We reseal the perimeter and flashing to stop water at the edge.

Valley failures

Roof valleys carry the most water. Worn valley flashing lets it through — we rebuild the valley to channel it away.

Plumbing stack boots

The rubber boots around plumbing vents crack and dry out over time. A worn boot is a slow, sneaky leak we replace fast.

Ice-dam damage

Ontario winters build ice dams that force water back under shingles. We repair the damage and add ice & water shield to prevent it.

Wind-lifted shingles

High winds lift and tear shingles, exposing the deck. We replace damaged shingles with colour-matched materials.
