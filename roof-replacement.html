FAQ

Common Questions About Roof Replacement

Most residential roof replacements in the GTA range from $8,000 to $18,000+ depending on the size of your home, roof pitch, number of layers to remove, and shingle choice. We provide a free, no-obligation inspection and detailed written quote so you know exactly what you're paying for — no ballpark estimates, no surprises.

Most standard residential roofs are completed in one day. Larger homes or more complex rooflines (multiple dormers, steep pitch, skylights) may take two days. We'll give you a realistic timeline upfront and stick to it — and we complete the job before we leave for the day.

If your roof was damaged by a covered event (hail, wind, fallen tree), your insurance may cover some or all of the replacement. We can document the damage and help you navigate the claims process. Age-related wear is typically not covered — but a free inspection will help you understand exactly where you stand.

We don't offer in-house financing at this time. However, many Toronto homeowners use a HELOC or home equity line of credit to fund roof replacement — your bank can typically process this quickly. We're happy to work around your timeline and provide documentation your bank may need.

Every replacement comes with a 10-year written workmanship warranty — if anything we installed causes a problem, we come back and fix it at no charge. You also receive the manufacturer's product warranty (Owens Corning and CertainTeed both offer limited lifetime warranties on qualifying shingles).
