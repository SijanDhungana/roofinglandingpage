Materials

Built for Ontario Winters

A roof is only as good as what goes under the shingles. We install premium, CSA-rated architectural shingles chosen for their performance through Ontario’s freeze-thaw cycles — the repeated freezing and thawing that cracks cheaper materials and lifts seams over a few winters.

Under them, we lay a full ice & water shield in the valleys and along the eaves — the zones where ice dams form — plus a synthetic underlayment and proper drip edge. It’s the difference between a roof that looks new for a season and one that stays watertight for decades.

We’ll walk you through the exact products and colour options in your written quote, so you know precisely what’s going on your home.

Ice & water shield

Applied in valleys and along eaves to stop ice-dam water forcing its way back under the shingles.

Full deck inspection

Once the old roof is off, we check the decking and replace any rotten sheathing before the new roof goes down.

Proper ventilation

Balanced intake and exhaust ventilation keeps your attic dry, prevents ice dams, and extends the life of the roof.
