About Us

Local Roofers You Can Trust

Optima Roofing & Construction was founded in London, Ontario with one simple idea: homeowners deserve a roofing company that shows up when it says it will, does what it promises, and stands behind its work.

We're a family-run crew — not a franchise, not a call centre. When you call us, you speak to the people who'll be on your roof. We've been protecting homes across Southwestern Ontario for over 15 years, and every job we take gets the same level of care.

Our 25-year workmanship warranty backs every replacement. We carry full WSIB coverage and $5M liability insurance so you're protected from day one.
